## 💡 Key Insights

### Size-Dependent Optimization

**Discovery**: Optimization effectiveness depends on problem scale

| System Size | Result | Reason |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| **< 10 hydros** | Regression | Overhead > Savings |
| **10-30 hydros** | Neutral | Transition zone |
| **> 30 hydros** | **WIN** | Savings > Overhead |

**Crossover**: ~20-30 hydros

**Implication**: Optimization helps production workloads, not toy examples

### Overhead Analysis

**Small allocations are FAST**:
- Allocating 12 f64 values: ~15ns
- Thread-local + RefCell access: ~50ns
- **For small Vecs, allocation wins!**

**Large allocations are SLOW**:
- Allocating 15,600 f64 values: ~30μs (2,000× slower)
- Buffer overhead: still ~50ns
- **For large Vecs, buffer reuse dominates!**

**Lesson**: **Context matters in optimization**

### Stability as a Benefit

**Unexpected win**: Optimized version is **much more stable**
- Baseline: 67-115s (72% variance)
- Optimized: 78-79s (0.7% variance)

**Why**: Consistent buffer reuse → predictable cache behavior
