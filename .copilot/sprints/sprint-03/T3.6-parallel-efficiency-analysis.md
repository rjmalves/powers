## Context

POWE.RS uses Rayon for thread-based parallelism in forward/backward passes. Current parallelism is **opportunistic** (added where easy) without analysis of efficiency. Understanding parallel scaling characteristics is critical for production deployment.

**Current State**:

- Rayon used for forward pass scenario parallelism
- Rayon used for backward pass scenario parallelism
- No measurement of parallel efficiency
- No analysis of lock contention or overhead
- Unknown optimal thread count

**Need**: Comprehensive analysis of parallel efficiency to validate current approach and identify bottlenecks.
